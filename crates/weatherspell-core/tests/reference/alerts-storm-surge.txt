Details: Storm surge warning

Storm surge warning from Environment Canada, in effect from 7:00 pm today until 5:00 pm tomorrow.

Area: Coastline from Mackenzie Bay to McKinley Bay incl. Tuktoyaktuk.

Issued 5:38 pm yesterday.

Yellow level, moderate impact, high confidence.

Coastal flooding due to storm surge is expected Sunday evening and early Monday morning in Tuktoyaktuk.

Moderate flooding is possible beginning late Sunday evening with water levels possibly reaching 85 cm above highest astronomical tide.

However, moderate flooding is more likely early Monday morning, where water levels could reach as high as 1 metre above highest astronomical tide.

Water levels will recede Monday evening.

This warning is issued when high water levels pose a threat to coastal regions due to tides, storm surge and/or waves.

Please continue to monitor alerts and forecasts issued by Environment Canada. To report severe weather, send an email to NTstorm@ec.gc.ca, call 1-800-239-0484 or post reports on social media using #NTStorm.

Details: Storm surge warning

Storm surge warning from Environment Canada, in effect until 2:00 pm Tuesday.

Area: Coastline of Yukon incl. Herschel Island.

Issued 5:46 pm yesterday.

Yellow level, moderate impact, high confidence.

A multi day storm surge event continues for Herschel Island.

Moderate flooding is expected at each high tide until early next week, with the highest water levels reaching 25 to 40 cm above highest astronomical tide in the mornings and afternoons.

These conditions will cause damage to infrastructure near the coast, including boats beached or anchored along the shoreline.

This warning is issued when high water levels pose a threat to coastal regions due to tides, storm surge and/or waves.
